Tu Hi Wo Haramkhor Hai 😤

Jai, har achhi cheez ke peeche tera haath hai. Aur har gandi cheez ke peeche bhi. Sun le apna kissa, madarchod.

01
Pehli baar sharab pilane wala tu hi hai. Us raat maine kasam khayi thi ki dobara nahi piyunga — aur agle hafte tu phir se bottle leke aa gaya. Haramkhor.
02
Mujhe bigaadne wala tu hi hai. Ghar walon ne mujhe sudharne bheja tha, aur tu ne mujhe aur bigaad diya. Kamine, teri wajah se hi main aaj aisa hoon.
03
Har galti mein tera hi haath hai. Main innocent tha, saala tu hi mujhe galat raaste pe le gaya — aur phir khud bach gaya. Bhosdike, tu hi asli mastermind hai.
04
Maa ne bola tha door reh usse. Par main bewakoof tha, tere saath chipak gaya. Aaj kaunse gaali kha raha hoon? Main. Aur blame bhi mera hi hai. Saale.
05
Tu wo haramkhor hai jiske saath main jail jaa sakta hoon — par pakka jaunga bhi, kyunki saala tu hi wajah hoga. Aur andar bhi mujhe hi maar khani padegi. Kutte.
06
Aur phir bhi... tu hi wo hai jise main sabse zyada miss karta hoon. Kutte, tu mera bhai nahi, par tu hi mera sab kuch hai. (Ye mat batana kisi ko, warna gaand maar dunga.)
